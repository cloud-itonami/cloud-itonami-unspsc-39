# Business Model: Independent Solar & EV-Charging Install & Diagnostics

## Classification

- Repository: `cloud-itonami-unspsc-39`
- UNSPSC segment: `39` (Electrical Systems and Lighting and Components and
  Accessories and Supplies)
- Activity: solar panel and EV-charger installation, diagnostics and
  commissioning
- Social impact: faster decarbonization adoption, safer electrical
  installs, local trade jobs

## Customer

- homeowners and small businesses installing solar or EV chargers
- property managers electrifying parking/fleet infrastructure
- solar/EV installers who want a governed diagnostics + commissioning tool
  instead of paper checklists

## Offer

- pre-install site survey and load assessment
- panel/charger fault diagnostics
- commissioning record with test-and-tag evidence
- live-circuit / grid-interconnection safety escalation (human sign-off,
  never automated)
- warranty and maintenance-history audit export

## Revenue

- per-install commissioning fee
- diagnostics-as-a-service for third-party installers
- maintenance subscription (periodic re-inspection)
- warranty-claim evidence package

## Trust Controls

- install/repair recommendations require Electrical Install Governor
  clearance
- any `:high`/`:safety-critical` action (live circuit, grid
  interconnection) always escalates to human sign-off
- every diagnostic run, finding and escalation is logged
- public performance/safety claims must reference measured sensor data
